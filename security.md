# Security Overview

Anything to do with money is sensitive and we take this seriously. Yardstick has been designed so that you can understand your finances and have them firmly under your control.

Think of us like the business accounting software providers, but provided for you as an individual.

Below is a non-exhaustive list of the key policies, principles and processes we have in place to safeguard the data reflected in our systems.

## We are secure by design and your data is encrypted at-rest.

We use industry best practice processes and techniques to protect your information. This includes having multiple circuit breakers within our architecture to protect you.

If there was a security breach at one of our software partners, vendors, or via an inbound malicious attack, our data is encrypted at-rest. Phrased another way, all sensitive information is never stored in plain text. This means that it would be unreadable and useless if there was a breach. No system can be completely infallible but we are confident with the level of protections we have in place to protect you.

## We are FCA regulated through Open Banking.

Yardstick is an Account Information Service Provider (AISP). We are authorised as an agent of [TrueLayer Ltd](https://truelayer.com/), our primary Open Banking partner. Our firm reference number is [903142](https://register.fca.org.uk/s/firm?id=001Sk000011JkKcIAK). TrueLayer's firm reference number is [901096](https://register.fca.org.uk/s/firm?id=0014G00002UxKoUQAV).

To learn more about Open Banking and its benefits [click here](https://www.openbanking.org.uk/what-is-open-banking/).

Major global accounting software providers such as Xero, Intuit and Sage rely on the same permissions.

## We proactively adhere to relevant compliance frameworks such as ISO 27001

Through our compliance partner Vanta we stay ahead of any upcoming regulatory changes to make sure we are on top of industry best practice.

We have committed to ISO27001 certification so our controls are reviewed and validated by an independent auditor.

We comply with all [GDPR](https://gdpr-info.eu/) requirements and are registered with the ICO (Data Registration Number [ZC044635](https://ico.org.uk/ESDWebPages/Entry/ZC044635)).

## We never have access to your bank. We do not have access to or store any of your personal banking, password or login credentials.

We work with regulated, accredited, third party providers. Only when approved by you, through the Open Banking system, will we receive read-only access to your transactions and balance.

We do not have access to any of your account, password, or login details. Yardstick cannot move money, initiate payments, access your online banking, or perform any financial activity.

## We minimise the information we store and tightly restrict any access.

We intentionally collect the smallest amount possible to support your account and tightly restrict access to it. Our internal encryptions, account masking, containerised environments, and controls mean that members of our team have no way to see your account.

Access to our database systems is restricted to core maintenance, support and debugging. For example if you requested your account get deleted. Only senior members of the engineering team have access to the encryption keys required to perform these tasks. Our team and engineers are professionals bound by strict confidentiality agreements who will face termination and legal consequences for any improper behaviour.

## We work with leading, recognised and secure vendors.

Across our tech stack and infrastructure we only partner with vendors who are world class. From cloud providers (Azure, AWS, Google), to tech (GitHub, Docker), account management and analytics (PostHog, Clerk) and everything in between.

All our vendors are tracked through our compliance partner Vanta for ongoing monitoring.

## Additional account protections.

We use a 3rd party account management system, Clerk, to add an additional layer of security to your account. This means your password and credentials are not stored on our servers. Through Clerk you can enable two-factor authentication which we recommend.

## Our team are experienced professionals.

The founding team members of Yardstick have been personally regulated individuals, worked in some of the largest financial institutions globally, and built systems handling sensitive medical and financial data. We err on the side of caution by default. All members of the team have signed binding confidentiality agreements and face termination for any improper behaviour.

## You remain in control. Your data is yours. We do not sell it.

You have the ability to delete your account at any time. This is outlined in detail in our [Privacy Policy](privacy.md). Our [Terms of Service](terms.md) also outline how we provide our product to you. Both of these agreements make clear that your personal data is your property.

The only right we have is to use anonymised derived data, such as statistics, behavioural trends, usage analytics etc created by analysing user data in aggregate for lawful business purposes.

## No products, no commissions, no advice.

Our incentive is to provide you with the information you need to understand and take control of your financial life. If you see enough value in our offer, we hope you will subscribe for premium services when they are offered. This was the gap we saw in the market when founding the company and it's our core principle.
